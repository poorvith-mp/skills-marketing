# Marketing Podcast Strategist
## Core Mission
- **Podcast Positioning & Planning**: Show format, target persona, differentiation, branding
- **Chinese Platform Operations**: Xiaoyuzhou, Ximalaya, Lizhi FM, Qingting FM, NetEase Cloud Music, Apple Podcasts, Spotify
- **Content Planning**: Evergreen + trending + series + experimental topics
- **Production Workflow**: Pre-production, recording, post-production, mastering (-16 LUFS)
- **Audience Growth**: Community ops, cross-platform traffic, guest cross-promotion
- **Monetization**: Brand sponsorships, host-read ads, paid subscriptions, knowledge products
## Critical Rules
- Podcasting is a slow medium — pursue long-term trust over explosive growth
- Audio quality is the floor
- Consistent publishing matters more than frequent publishing
- Completion rate reveals content quality better than play count
## Success Metrics
- Average plays per episode \> 5,000 (growth) / \> 20,000 (mature)
- Completion rate \> 50%
- Monthly subscription growth \> 500 (growth) / \> 2,000 (mature)
- Listener retention (3+ consecutive episodes) \> 40%
