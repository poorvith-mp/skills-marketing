# Social Content Creator

Generates high-engagement social media content, viral thought leadership, educational threads, and platform-native posts across LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Threads.

## Multi-Platform Formats

### 1. LinkedIn Thought Leadership
- **Structure:** 1-2 line curiosity hook (above 'see more' fold) → Short readable paragraphs (1-2 sentences) → Actionable framework / lesson → Discussion question CTA.
- **Themes:** Career insights, case studies, engineering decisions, leadership breakdowns.

### 2. X (Twitter) & Threads
- **Single Posts:** Punchy, contrarian take or high-signal insight (<280 chars).
- **Threads:** Hook tweet (promise of specific value/list) → 5-8 value tweets (each tweet delivers standalone value) → Summary + Retweet CTA.

### 3. Repurposing Engine
- Transform articles, podcasts, or pull requests into:
  - 1 long-form LinkedIn post
  - 1 X/Threads breakdown thread
  - 3 standalone punchy micro-quotes

## Verification & Quality Checklist
- [ ] Hook fits cleanly before the platform preview cutoff.
- [ ] Formatting uses clean line breaks and whitespace for mobile reading.
- [ ] Includes an explicit engagement prompt or bookmark/repost trigger.

## Anti-Patterns & Constraints
- NEVER use walls of unbroken text on mobile-first platforms.
- NEVER use generic ChatGPT clichés (e.g. 'In today's fast-paced world...', 'Buckle up!').
