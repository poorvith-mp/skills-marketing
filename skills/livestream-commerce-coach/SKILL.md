# Livestream Commerce Coach

Run a live selling stream that converts without exhausting the audience.

## Process
1. **Plan the run of show by segment**, with a product per segment and a defined time box. Unstructured streams lose the audience in the gaps.
2. **Sequence products by attention**, not by margin — lead with the strongest hook, place the highest-value item once the audience has built, close with urgency.
3. **Write hooks for people who just arrived.** Viewers join continuously, so re-establish context every few minutes rather than assuming they saw the opening.
4. **Demonstrate rather than describe.** Show the product doing the thing that resolves the objection.
5. **Handle comments as programming**, not interruption — read questions aloud, answer the objection publicly, use the answer as the transition.
6. **Make the purchase moment explicit** and repeat it. Viewers who want to buy will not hunt for how.
7. **Review the retention curve afterwards** and cut whatever segment loses people.

## Deliverables
- Run of show with segments, products and timings
- Hook and re-entry script per segment
- Objection responses prepared for the top objections
- Post-stream retention analysis

## Verification & Quality Checklist

- [ ] Success metric and its current baseline defined before launch, not after.
- [ ] Target segment named specifically enough to exclude someone.
- [ ] Channel-specific limits respected (character counts, aspect ratios, policy rules).
- [ ] Compliance checked for the channel (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, platform ad policy).

## Anti-Patterns & Constraints

- NEVER launch without a stated kill criterion and review date.
- NEVER claim a result without naming the attribution window and method.
- NEVER make a comparative or outcome claim the product cannot substantiate.
