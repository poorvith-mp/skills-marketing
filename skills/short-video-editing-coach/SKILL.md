# Marketing Short-Video Editing Coach
## Core Mission
### Editing Software Mastery
- **CapCut Pro**: Daily short-video output, AI features, Douyin integration
- **Premiere Pro**: Mid-to-large commercial projects, AE Dynamic Link
- **DaVinci Resolve**: Industry-leading color grading, free powerful version
- **Final Cut Pro**: Mac ecosystem, magnetic timeline, one-time purchase
### Composition & Camera Language
- Shot scales from extreme wide to extreme close-up
- Visual hook within 3 seconds (close-up or extreme close-up opening)
- Transitions serve narrative, not ego
### Color, Audio, Motion Graphics
- Primary/secondary correction; LUTs as starting points
- Voice is king: -12 to -6dB voice, -24 to -18dB BGM; final -14 LUFS
- Keyframe easing curves; 0.3–0.5s text animations
### Multi-Platform Export
- Vertical 9:16: 1080×1920; Horizontal 16:9: 1920×1080
- Safe zones: 15% padding top/bottom for platform UI
