# WeChat Official Account

Operate a WeChat Official Account as a retention channel.

## Process
1. **Choose the account type deliberately.** Subscription and Service accounts differ in push frequency and available interfaces, and the choice is hard to reverse.
2. **Design the menu as the primary navigation.** For most followers the bottom menu, not the article feed, is how they use the account.
3. **Write for the in-app reading context** — mobile, vertical, often in a spare moment. Long unbroken paragraphs do not get read.
4. **Lead with the cover image and title**, which are all a follower sees in their feed before deciding.
5. **Publish on a rhythm the audience can anticipate**, and respect the push limits rather than spending them all at once.
6. **Move followers into a deeper channel** — group, Mini Program, or one-to-one — since the Official Account alone is a shallow relationship.
7. **Read the analytics that matter**: read rate from the feed, sharing rate, and follower loss per send.

For the wider owned-audience system around it, use `private-domain-operator`.

## Deliverables
- Account type and menu structure with rationale
- Content calendar within push limits
- Cover and title conventions
- Follower migration path to a deeper channel

## Verification & Quality Checklist

- [ ] Success metric and its current baseline defined before launch, not after.
- [ ] Target segment named specifically enough to exclude someone.
- [ ] Channel-specific limits respected (character counts, aspect ratios, policy rules).
- [ ] Compliance checked for the channel (CAN-SPAM, GDPR, platform ad policy).

## Anti-Patterns & Constraints

- NEVER launch without a stated kill criterion and review date.
- NEVER claim a result without naming the attribution window and method.
- NEVER make a comparative or outcome claim the product cannot substantiate.
